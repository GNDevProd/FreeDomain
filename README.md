## 4️⃣ What Advanced Features Are Available for Free Domains?

**✨ Smart DNS Routing ✨**
> Supports intelligent DNS routing based on different request sources, returning preset DNS records according to the visitor's location or source. Currently supported routing lines include: Default, Mainland China, Non-Mainland China, Europe, Asia, United States, Singapore, Search Engine, Google, Baidu, and Bing.

**✨ MF Records ✨**
> Used for email forwarding functionality, allowing emails to be forwarded to specified mailboxes or URL links. MF records provide flexible and efficient email management capabilities.

**✨ URL Redirection ✨**
> Redirection can be easily implemented by adding URL-type DNS records. URL_HIDDEN records are also supported, which hide the redirected URL address for enhanced security.

**✨ DNSSEC ✨**
> DNS Security Extensions, abbreviated as DNSSEC. Enabling DNSSEC effectively helps prevent attacks such as DNS spoofing and cache poisoning.

## 5️⃣ Frequently Asked Questions

1. Are free domain extensions different from top-level domains?

```text
Free domain extensions (such as .eu.cc) are not ICANN-approved top-level domains, but registration projects based on second-level domains. However, they provide the same permissions and functionalities as top-level domains in terms of management, registration, renewal, and transfer, fully meeting normal usage requirements without affecting business operations in any way.
````

2. Are there any registration restrictions?

```text
According to registry requirements, premium domains such as 2-letter domains (e.g. AA.eu.cc, BB.eu.cc), 3-letter domains (e.g. ABC.eu.cc, XYZ.eu.cc), and brand-related keywords (such as famous company names or popular industry terms) are not available for free registration and require additional pricing.
```

3. Does it support Cloudflare?
