# GNAME Free Domain Registration Beginner Guide

## 1. Register a GNAME Account

Visit **www.gname.com** and click the registration button in the navigation bar. You can register a GNAME account using your email address or third-party accounts (such as Facebook, Github, etc.).

![register](./register.png)

## 2. Search for a Free Domain

Open the free domain registration page: **https://www.gname.com/tld-eu-cc.html**

Enter the domain name you wish to register, click search, and find your preferred domain from the search results.

> 【Tips】
>
> Each new user can receive **3 free registration coupons**, which will be automatically deducted during checkout 🎉🎉🎉🎉🎉🎉
>
> Additional fees may apply for premium domains. Premium domains include, but are not limited to: 2-letter domains (such as AA.eu.cc, BB.eu.cc), 3-letter domains (such as ABC.eu.cc, XYZ.eu.cc), and brand-related keywords (such as famous company names or popular industry terms).

![search_domain](./search_domain.png)

![settlement](./settlement.png)

**Additional profile information is required for first-time registration**

![complete_info](./complete_info.png)

Your domain will be successfully registered after payment is completed.

## 3. Domain DNS Management

GNAME provides free DNS servers. You can add DNS records in the control panel under -> My Domains.

You may also use third-party DNS services. Click "Manage" to modify DNS servers, such as Cloudflare and others.

## 4. Free Renewal

> 【Tips】
>
> Domains can only submit a free renewal request within 90 days before expiration through the “Free Renewal” list.
>
> Free renewal requests are only supported on the “Free Registration” campaign page. Free renewal is not available through the My Domains list, automatic renewal pages, or other related pages.
>
> Premium domains currently do not support free renewal. Actual renewal pricing is subject to the query result. Premium domains include 2-letter domains (such as AA.eu.cc, BB.eu.cc), 3-letter domains (such as ABC.eu.cc, XYZ.eu.cc), and brand-related keywords (such as famous company names or popular industry terms).
